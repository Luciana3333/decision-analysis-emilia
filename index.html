1. Select a preloaded example or enter your own problem

Choose an example to load a complete dataset, including probabilities. Or edit the table directly. Add or remove alternatives and states of nature as needed.

2. State probabilities

Probabilities must be between 0 and 1 and sum to 1.00 (100%) for EMV/Bayes. The other three criteria can still be calculated if probabilities are incomplete.

3. Decision criteria — automatic comparison

Results recalculate when you edit a payoff, alternative, state, probability, or objective. All ties are identified.

4. Staged filtering

Filtering applies one criterion at a time to the alternatives that survived the previous stage. At each stage, only the best score(s) remain. Tied alternatives are all retained. Change the order to explore a different sequence.

5. Staged-filtering flow diagram

The diagram follows the selected criterion order and shows the survivors and eliminated alternatives at each step.
